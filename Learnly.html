About Learnly
Version 1.0.0 · Learn with Leo!
Learnly is an educational app for children Pre-K through Grade 5. Created to make learning fun through games, encouragement, and a friendly mascot — Leo the Lion.
🔒 Privacy Policy
Data storage: All data (names, scores, progress) is stored only on this device using the browser's localStorage. We do not collect, transmit, or share any data with any server or third party.

No accounts required: No email, password, or personal information is required to use Learnly.

Children's privacy (COPPA): Learnly is designed for children under 13. We collect no personal information. No internet connection is required to play. No analytics or tracking code is included.

GDPR (EU users): No personal data leaves your device. There is nothing to request, export, or delete beyond clearing your browser's localStorage.

Data deletion: Parents can delete all data at any time via Parent Settings → Reset ALL data.

📖 Parent Guide — Getting Started
🚀 New here? Start in 3 steps
Set up a kid profile → Pick their grade → Hand them the device. That's it!
Step-by-step setup
1
Add a child profile — tap "+ Add a Child" above. Give them a name and pick a fun emoji avatar. You can add multiple kids and switch between them anytime.
2
Set their grade level — use "Max Level" above to cap the difficulty. A 4-year-old should be on Pre-K; a 6-year-old on Kindergarten or Grade 1.
3
Choose subjects — toggle Math, Reading, and Science on or off. All three are on by default and recommended for Pre-K through Grade 2.
4
Set a session limit — 15–20 minutes is ideal for young kids. The app will pause automatically when time is up.
5
Set a PIN — this prevents kids from changing settings. Default PIN is 1234 — change it to something only you know.
6
Pick a voice — use "Pick Voice" above to preview voices and choose one that sounds friendly and clear on your device.
What's in the app?
🔢 Math
Counting (tap to count objects) · Adding (visual group addition) · Shapes (name the shape) · Big & Small (compare quantities)
📖 Reading
Letters (identify A–Z with phonics) · Words (match simple words to pictures)
🔬 Science
Colors · Animals (sounds & habitats) · Weather · Sink or Float experiments
🚀 Unlockable Subjects (Grade 2+)
Multiplication · Geography · Earth Science · Fractions — these unlock automatically as the grade level increases.
🎮 Fun Zone Games
Kids unlock games by earning stars: Slide Puzzle (10⭐) · Basketball (25⭐) · Balloon Pop (40⭐) · Baseball (60⭐) · Checkers (80⭐) · Chess (120⭐). Games are a reward for learning!
🏆 Leaderboard & Groups
Create friend groups and compare scores. Use "Export" to share a code with another device, and "Import" to load their scores into your leaderboard. Great for cousins or classmates competing!
Curriculum guide
K
Pre-K / Kindergarten — Focus on Counting (1–9), Letters, Colors, and Animal sounds. Run 2–3 short sessions per day.
1
Grade 1 — Add shapes and simple addition. Introduce weather and sink-or-float experiments to build curiosity.
2
Grade 2 — All core subjects + Multiplication unlocks. Start using the leaderboard to build motivation.
3+
Grade 3–5 — Geography, Earth Science, and Fractions become available. Great for reinforcing what's covered in school.
